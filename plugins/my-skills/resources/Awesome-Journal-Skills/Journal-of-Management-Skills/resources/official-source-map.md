Rosen holds the William R. & Cacilia Howard Chair in Management and previously served as JOM Associate Editor. Re-verified 2026-09-14 via University of Arkansas news announcement. Sources: **https://news.uark.edu/articles/82720/christopher-rosen-named-editor-in-chief-of-ft50-journal-of-management** (U of Arkansas Walton College, "Christopher Rosen Named Editor-in-Chief of FT50 Journal of Management"; accessed 2026-09-14).
